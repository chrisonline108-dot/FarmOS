Fertility / treatment protocol

Original farm protocol: cow/chicken/possibly horse manure + compost + wood ash + roasted coffee shells, with compost at transplanting and a liquid mixture using compost tea, seaweed, humic acid, molasses/sugar, vermicompost and coffee material. The operating plan below refines that approach.
1
Test first
Soil and irrigation water testing before major fertility decisions.
2
Organic matter
Use verified mature/treated compost or manure-based compost at measured rates; correct compaction and drainage.
3
Coffee / ash
Co-compost coffee chaff. Do not routinely use wood ash unless soil analysis supports it.
4
Optional inputs
Seaweed and humic products only as measured optional inputs.
5
Food safety
Avoid routine homemade compost-tea/molasses foliar applications on raw-eaten crops.
6
Review timing
Fertility review around day 21; selected crops reviewed again around day 42.
7
Pest management
Scouting-based: observe, identify, record, then use an appropriate organic-compatible treatment only when justified.
8
Current treatment inventory names
Azaxol · Insectazol · Silazelle · Spinozad · Bt · Soap · Pepper · Neem oil. Product rate, crop approval, PHI/REI and expiry should be recorded from each label before use.
9
Current fertilizer / amendment inventory names
Vermi compost · Compost · Humic acid · Sugar · Seaweed · Beneficial bacteria · Weeds · Sheepwhool pellets · Wood Ash · Coffee eshre.

Recordkeeping & data model

Current structured records
Excel / spreadsheets for beds, planting dates, harvest windows, nursery starts, fertility reviews and daily tasks.
Current gap
No single integrated database yet connecting crops, harvests, inventory, chickens, labour, sales and tasks.
Core operating chain
Bed → Crop → Task → Input → Harvest → Inventory → Sale.
Crop record states
Use Planned for intended crops, Active for physically planted/current crops, and Finished after harvest/clearing.

Core system priorities

1. What is planted where and when
Exact bed, crop, planting date, harvest and replacement crop.
2. Succession & nursery timing
Start the next tray/direct sowing before harvest gaps appear.
3. Harvest, sales & waste
Track what was harvested, sold, used by the café, wasted or remains in stock.
4. Inventory & procurement
Avoid running out of seeds, seedlings, compost, treatments, irrigation parts, feed, packaging and tools.
5. Recurring duties
Every farm and chicken task needs a responsible person and due date.
